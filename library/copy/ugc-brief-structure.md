# UGC brief structure

A creator or UGC brief gives a non-professional enough structure to deliver the concept and enough freedom to sound like themselves. Every field below is required; a worked example for Two Wrenches Plumbing (Dale and Priya) follows, and the rules it leans on are in `../platform/creator-category-truth-rules.md` and `../platform/ftc-endorsements.md`.

## Persona and situation

Who the creator is playing, or who they are if they are a real customer, and the moment the ad starts in. Pull the situation from the persona file (`../rubrics/persona-schema.md`), in the persona's words. A real customer only says what happened to them.

## The single angle

One angle from `angle-families.md`, stated as a sentence. If the brief needs two angles it is two briefs.

## Three hook options

Three hooks from `hook-taxonomy.md`, each written out, each tagged with its type. The creator records all three; the test plan decides which runs.

## The body beats

Four beats in order: problem, mechanism, proof, first step. Each beat is one or two sentences of guidance, not a script. The creator says it their way. Proof must be something the creator can truthfully show or say.

## The on-screen text plan

What text appears and when: the hook card, the beat labels, the proof caption, the CTA card. Keep each under eight words and inside the safe zones in `../platform/specs-and-safe-zones.md`.

## The CTA

One CTA pattern from `cta-patterns.md`, with the exact destination named.

## What not to say

Policy lines and claims without proof, as a short checklist: no results or income promises, no second-person health or financial attributes, no before-and-after in restricted categories, no "guaranteed," nothing the member cannot document, no scripted lines passed off as the creator's own experience.

## The "ugly is fine" note

A sentence telling the creator that phone footage, natural light and a cluttered background are expected (see `../strategy/hott-ugly-ads.md`). Captions and audible speech are the only quality bars.

## Shot list

Five to ten shots, each one line: what is in frame, what happens, rough length. Include at least one shot that proves the mechanism and one that shows the first step.

## Deliverables and aspect ratios

File count, aspect ratios (9:16 required; 4:5 and 1:1 if the test plan uses feed placements), length range, raw footage or cut, caption file, and the disclosure line the creator includes if there is a material connection.

## Rights and disclosure

Usage term, platforms, whether the member may edit, and the disclosure wording. Consent on file before anything runs.

## Example brief: Two Wrenches Plumbing

**Persona and situation.** The creator is Marcus, a real customer (consent on file) who owns a 1970s house. The ad starts the morning he found water under the stairs.

**The single angle.** Mechanism-led: nine heater models on the truck is why a same-day swap is possible, and most shops order yours after they visit.

**Three hook options.**
1. Story opener: "Thursday, 6:40am, water under the stairs, and I had a tenant's cheque to deposit at nine."
2. Specific number: "Nine water heaters on one truck. I counted."
3. Confession or pratfall: "I tried the drain cleaner first. Priya showed me what it did to the pipe."

**The body beats.**
- Problem: say what you found and what you thought it would cost in time.
- Mechanism: say that Dale had the replacement on the truck and show the rack if you filmed it.
- Proof: show your receipt with the date and time, and the dry cabinet now.
- First step: say you texted a photo of the label and got a price back before the kids left for school.

**On-screen text plan.** Hook card, first second. "Same morning" over the receipt shot. "Text a photo of the label" over the last shot.

**CTA.** Small first step: "Text a photo of your heater label to the number on the profile. Priya replies with a price."

**What not to say.** No "fastest in town." No "always same day" (it is most calls, not all). No health or safety scare lines. Do not name the brand of heater as the reason it failed.

**Ugly is fine.** Film it on your phone in the hallway. The peeling van lettering can be in shot.

**Shot list.**
1. You at the top of the stairs, pointing down. 3s.
2. The old heater before removal, if you have the photo. 2s.
3. Dale's truck rack with the heaters. 3s.
4. The new heater in place. 3s.
5. Your receipt, date and time visible. 2s.
6. You in the kitchen, hot tap running. 3s.
7. The text thread with Priya, your number blurred. 3s.

**Deliverables and aspect ratios.** Three raw takes (one per hook), 9:16, 25 to 45 seconds each, plus the seven shots as separate clips; 4:5 crop of the best take; caption file. Include the line "Two Wrenches did my repair at a discount for this video."

**Rights and disclosure.** Twelve months, Meta and TikTok, member may cut and caption, disclosure line stays in the caption and on screen.
