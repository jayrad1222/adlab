# Copy formulas

The copywriter agent uses these as scaffolds for body copy and leads, never as fill-in templates. Each formula lists its beats and one short original example for Two Wrenches Plumbing (Dale and Priya) or First Rental (a course for self-managing one rental unit); every example passes Harry Dry's three tests (`harry-dry-three-tests.md`).

## AIDA

Beats: attention, interest, desire, action. Works when the audience is solution-aware or beyond and the ad has room for all four beats.

Example: "Your water heater is 14 years old. (attention) Most fail between years 10 and 15, and they fail on a Sunday. (interest) Dale can swap it for a tank that is in stock on the truck, same afternoon. (desire) Text a photo of the label and Priya replies with a price before you finish your coffee. (action)"

## PAS (problem, agitate, solve)

Beats: name the problem, make its cost vivid, present the solution. The default for problem-aware audiences. Agitation must be true and fair.

Example: "The first-month rent cheque arrived with a note: the dryer vent is blocked. (problem) Now you are googling whether that is your job or the tenant's, at 10pm, with no lease clause that says. (agitate) First Rental's lesson four is the twelve clauses that settle this before move-in, with a lease you can edit tonight. (solve)"

## BAB (before, after, bridge)

Beats: the current state, the future state, the product as the bridge. A Gap Selling structure in miniature; see `../sales/gap-selling.md`. The "after" must be a state the member can honestly deliver, not a result they promise.

Example: "Before: three quote requests sitting in your inbox from Saturday. After: each one answered with a price and a date. Bridge: Priya's two-line quote template, which she will send you if you text QUOTE."

## The 4 Ps (picture, promise, prove, push)

Beats: paint the scene, promise the outcome, prove it, push to action. Useful for video scripts because each beat maps to a shot.

Example: "Picture: a crawlspace with six inches of standing water. Promise: Dale finds the break in under an hour on most calls. Prove: here is the camera scope footage from Thursday's job on Elm Street, pipe and all. Push: the scope visit is $89 and comes off the repair."

## Star-Story-Solution

Beats: introduce the star (a customer, the founder, the viewer), tell the story of the problem, arrive at the solution. The star must be a real person or clearly the viewer.

Example: "Nadia bought a duplex in March and lost the first tenant in June over a late repair she did not know was hers. She wrote down every question she wished she had asked. That list became First Rental, and lesson one is the question she missed."

## The open loop lead

Beats: an unresolved statement, a reason to keep watching, the resolution placed after the mechanism. Sugarman's seed of curiosity as a lead. Pay it off or cut it.

Example: "The part that caused the leak cost four dollars. The part Dale replaced instead cost nothing, and I will show you why in a second."

## The "you've probably tried" lead (accusation audit)

Beats: name what the viewer has already tried, acknowledge why it failed, then show the difference. Voss's accusation audit; see `../sales/voss-toolkit.md`. Best objection-handling lead in the library.

Example: "You have probably already bought a lease template, read it once, and never opened it again. That is not a discipline problem. The template did not tell you which clauses matter in your state. First Rental does, by state, in lesson four."

## The mechanism reveal lead

Beats: the result, then the "how" that nobody else explains, then the proof that the mechanism exists. The stage-three sophistication move; see `../strategy/schwartz-market-sophistication.md`.

Example: "Dale carries nine water heater models on the truck. That is the whole reason a same-day swap is possible. Here is the rack."

## The confession lead (pratfall)

Beats: admit a real flaw or a past mistake, let it raise the credibility of the rest, then make the claim. Works only with a true flaw; see `../psychology/shotton-choice-factory.md`.

Example: "Our van is 2009 and the lettering is peeling. We spent the money on the scope camera instead. You will see why on the first visit."

## The specific number lead

Beats: one verifiable number in the first line, the context that makes it meaningful, the claim it supports. The number comes from the member's records.

Example: "Priya has quoted 312 water heater jobs since 2023. Here is what she has learned from the 11 we got wrong."

## The identification lead

Beats: name the reader so precisely that the wrong reader scrolls on, then speak to their situation. Caples' identification headline and Hormozi's callout in one.

Example: "If you own exactly one rental, live more than an hour from it, and your tenant has your personal number, First Rental was built for you."
