# Lesson 3: personas to angles (12 minutes)

**Screen:** a persona file, then concepts/matrix.md.

1. A persona is a situation, not a segment. Read one: the push, the pull, the anxiety, the habit. Show the language bank and where each phrase came from.
2. The review step: cross out the persona you would not sell to. Show the revision keeping the old version.
3. The interrogation: the diagnosis (which of the nine problems your business really has) and one of the seven recommendations with its mechanism, evidence, cheapest test and falsifier. Say why a reframe without a test is just an opinion.
4. The opposite pass: take the category's loudest convention, invert it, and ask whether the inverse is also a good idea.
5. The coverage matrix: persona by awareness stage by angle family. Show rejected cells with reasons. Say: the angle is the unit; hooks come after.
6. Pick one concept brief and read it top to bottom: the one idea, the mechanism, the competitor ads it answers and how it differs, the proof it can use, the six hooks, the critic's note.

**Post in the thread:** your strongest angle in one sentence and the competitor ad id it answers.
