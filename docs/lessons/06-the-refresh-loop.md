# Lesson 6: the refresh loop (6 minutes)

**Screen:** analysis/refresh-<date>.md.

1. The market moves. Run `/adlab:refresh` monthly or when a competitor launches something: it re-pulls the roster including stopped ads and diffs.
2. The four lists: new ads, ads that stopped, ads crossing 30, 60 and 90 days, new advertisers using your category's words. Why "stopped" is not "failed".
3. Send only the genuinely new ads through the rubric; append to ads.csv; update the market map's counts.
4. Feed the loop: a readout plus a refresh is a new round of angles. Retire concepts the readout killed; promote the ones that held.
5. The librarian: if you choose to, record observed patterns with ad ids so the library grows. Default is off; your business details never go in.
