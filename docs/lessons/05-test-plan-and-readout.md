# Lesson 5: the test plan and your first readout (10 minutes)

**Screen:** plan/economics.md, analysis/critic-report.md, then a readout.

1. finance.md: the few inputs that matter and why placeholders give placeholder answers. Price, margin, and either trial-to-paid plus months retained or AOV.
2. economics.md: breakeven CPA, target CPA, test budget per concept, how many concepts your monthly budget can carry to a verdict. Read the formula lines aloud once. Say what the number cannot tell you.
3. The structure: one campaign, one broad ad set, the ready concepts, three to six hooks each, no interest stacking. Why distinct concepts beat twenty variants of one idea.
4. The reading order: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, then CPA at volume. Thresholds are starting points you replace after your first readouts.
5. Before anything runs: `/adlab:review`. Open `analysis/critic-report.md`, read the verdict line, then the open items. Fix what it names with the command it names, and run it again until the overall verdict is pass.
6. Run the ads yourself. After a week, export at the ad level with the columns the skill names, then `/adlab:readout export.csv`. Show the rule that fired per ad and the action.
7. Say the quiet part: nothing was a winner until this readout, and a concept that fails hook rate is a cheap lesson about the angle, not a failure of the copy.
