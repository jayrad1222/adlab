# Lesson 2: reading your market map (10 minutes)

**Screen:** analysis/market-map.md from a finished run.

1. Where the roster came from: three rings (direct, indirect, attention) and why the attention ring matters in a feed.
2. The pull: what loaded versus what Meta reports, the per-advertiser cap, and the co-branded trick (a creator's Page can show zero while the platform runs their ads).
3. The sophistication stage. Read the stage the map called and the move it requires. Explain the five stages in a minute with your category as the example.
4. Table stakes versus uncontested claims. Point at the counts and the ad ids behind them. Say: you must say the table stakes, you cannot win on them.
5. The longevity leaderboard, and why the caveats are printed on it. An ad that ran 200 days ran 200 days.
6. Persona coverage: who every competitor talks to and who nobody does. This is where next week's angles come from.
7. Evidence limits: what the run did not inspect. Honest beats complete.

**Post in the thread:** your category's sophistication stage and the one claim nobody in your market is making.
