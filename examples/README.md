# Example runs

Two complete runs, from intake to test plan, so a member can read the output before installing anything.

- [operators-workshop/](operators-workshop/): a $97/month Skool community teaching practical AI. Intake from the About page plus the founders' standing brief; interrogation answers are defaults plus owner-provided facts, labeled.
- [ridge-wallet/](ridge-wallet/): a stand-in product brand (minimalist metal wallets). Nothing is owner-provided; everything is observed from Ridge's public pages (including its returns, warranty and product pages, captured for the angles step) or marked default.

## How to read one

1. `analysis/market-map.md`: who advertises, what everyone says, what nobody says, and the evidence limits.
2. `personas/`: the buying situations, with `coverage.md` showing who already talks to each.
3. `business/interrogation.md`: the diagnosis and seven recommendations, each with its test and falsifier. The critic blocked version 1 of both; version 2 answers every note, and the notes and replies are kept at the end.
4. `concepts/`: the coverage matrix, the opposite pass, and one brief per concept with the critic's note and the author's reply in each.
5. `bank/ad-bank.html`: open it in a browser. Every entry shows its hook, copy, reasoning, competitor references, risk flags and the critic's note; filter by persona, concept, format or status. `bank/ad-bank.csv` is the same bank as a spreadsheet; `bank/renders/` holds the statics rendered at 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.
6. `plan/test-plan.md`: what to run first, what to read, and the decision rules, with every placeholder labeled.
7. `analysis/critic-report.md`: the release review of the whole run: what passed, what is held and why, and the evidence limits that bind it.

`PROGRESS.md` shows which steps ran and `changelog.md` shows the order.

## What "ready", "held" and "blocked" mean here

Most entries are not ready, on purpose. Neither example has an asset catalog (nobody supplied photos, screens or consented quotes), so anything that needs a real picture, a real screen, a real person or a real customer's words waits for it and says what it waits for. A ready entry has passed the critic and still lists its run conditions (for Operators Workshop, the About-page edits the interrogation asks for; for Ridge, the dated terms and a standard-finish landing). An editorial score is a recording priority, never a prediction.

## What is not in the repo

Competitor creatives, frames and landing screenshots stay out of git (see `.gitignore`); the analysis cites the public ad library ids instead. Both runs were produced on 2026-10-23 with caps of 15 ads per advertiser to keep them readable; counts are samples, not totals. Neither run has transcripts (every video was read from its stills), and only run B has any customer language: five featured reviews from Ridge's own product page, used as insight and never as ad copy.
