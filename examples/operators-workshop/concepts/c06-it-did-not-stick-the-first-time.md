## Angle family

story-or-origin (the matrix column `story`)

## The one idea

Open on a result the founders rejected in their own build, then show what they changed, so the dabbler sees a setback from the founders' side before any pitch.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: the dabbler must feel "it is not just me" before any pitch. Their anxiety is that it did not stick because of them (p6 anxiety, inferred); a founder's rejected result may move the setback from the person to the process (hypothetical).
- Pratfall (`library/psychology/shotton-choice-factory.md`): an admitted flaw can raise trust when it is real; a fake one reads as the trick it is.
- Costly signaling (`library/psychology/costly-signaling.md`): opening an ad on a rejected result is a cost a course seller would not pay.
- Story (`library/psychology/heath-success.md`, stories; `library/copy/angle-families.md`, story or origin): a rejected result and a change is a turn the viewer can run in their head.
- Peak-end (`library/psychology/peak-end-rule.md`): the change is the last beat, so what is remembered is the recovery, not the mess. Without the recovery it may read as incompetence (hypothetical).
- Make the invisible visible (`library/psychology/make-the-invisible-visible.md`): the screen recording shows the work, the rejected part included.
- Copy move: frame one is the rejected result, dated and captioned "demonstration", with a kicker saying it comes from the founders of Operators Workshop, an AI community. Beat two says why it was rejected and what changed. The last beat shows the changed version, also captioned "demonstration". No line says it now runs in a business. No hook names the viewer's stall. No question about the viewer's personal attributes.
