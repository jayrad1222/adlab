# c03: AI ads are made for desks

Status: draft. Version 2, 2026-10-23. Version 1's changed wording is in the changelog at the end.

## Awareness stage

unaware. The concept fills matrix cell p2 x unaware x identity, which is open: in the copy, on-screen text and stills read, 0 of 12 roster advertisers address the field or trade owner; audio was not heard (market map section 5, inferred from the rows). The identity angle is run by 2 of 12 (`analysis/stats-roster.md`, angle_family), aimed at remote workers and non-coders. p2 is largely unaware that AI applies to a trade business at all (`personas/coverage.md`, awareness table, inferred), so the ad mirrors who they are before it says anything about AI. A problem-led ad would ask them to read AI into a problem they do not connect with it (matrix, rejected as wrong stage).

## Angle family

identity

## The one idea

AI ads show a laptop at a desk; this community is built by people who run businesses, one of them a fabrication shop, and it is about running the business, not about selling AI.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: the trade owner must feel "this was made by people who work like me" before reading a word about AI. The ad must not make them look like a student or like someone who has been doing it wrong (p2 emotional identity, inferred).
- Mirror (`library/strategy/levinger-three-realities-teep.md`): the viewer has to see their own world in the first seconds. A truck, a quote and a shop are that world; a desk is not.
- People like us (`library/psychology/godin-people-like-us.md`): name the group by what it does, not by a demographic; the file's own example is "for trades who quote on their phone".
- Context changes value (`library/psychology/context-changes-value.md`): the place named in the first line changes what the product is taken to be. A fabrication shop may move it out of the course category (hypothetical).
- Status and identity (`library/psychology/status-and-identity.md`): the status this owner wants is running a tighter operation than their peers (interrogation answer 1, owner-provided); the founders appear as operators, not presenters.
- Copy move: the hook names a place and an object from the owner's day (the cab, the quote) or the founders' business types. The same frame always carries the founders' fact and the words "AI community", so no hook stands alone. The body says the community is about running a business, in the About page's words, and sells no route to clients. No question about the viewer's personal attributes.

## Six hooks

1. (`identification`) "For the owner who types quotes in the truck between jobs."
2. (`authority`) "One of the three people behind this AI community runs a fabrication shop."
3. (`specific-number`) "Three founders. Between them, an online shop, a fabrication shop and an operations software company."
4. (`pov`) "POV: the quote gets typed in the cab before the next job."
5. (`pattern-interrupt`) "An AI ad with no laptop in it, from a community where one founder runs a fabrication shop."
6. (`question`) "Quote, schedule change, supplier question: which one waits until tonight?"

Hooks 2, 3 and 5 name a founder's business type and say "runs" in the present tense; they wait on both flags below. Hooks 1, 4 and 6 name no business, but the frames they run in carry the founders' fact in the body, so they share the same gates.

## The CTA

Button: `Learn more`. An unaware viewer is not ready for `Sign up`. Self-selection pattern (`library/copy/cta-patterns.md`): "Read who runs it before anything else. The About page names the three founders. 7 days free, then $97 a month." The trial is named, not pushed.

## Landing continuation

The click goes to the About page (`business/site/about/landing/page.txt`). Its first line is "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less." The second paragraph repeats the ad's "who" ("We're Jared, Julianna and Daniel. We run real businesses") but names no trade and no shop, so continuity is weak, not broken. A line naming the three businesses would make it strong, if that founder agrees to be named with their. Three About-page lines conflict with this concept and are listed in the interrogation's "Before any test runs": "earn more" (an income implication), "even after it rises to $129" (an urgency line) and "Get direct answers and support from us" (close to the support promises the standing brief rules out). The founders decide those edits.

- Staged or generated scenes: the statics carry no image. The mini-documentary must be real footage of the real shop; no AI-generated shop, person or voice (`library/platform/meta-policy-pitfalls.md`, AI-generated content).
