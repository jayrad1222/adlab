## What this pass rejected and why

- The small dated member count as a cold-traffic hook: a mechanism exists (precise numbers), but for a cold viewer five members reads as "nobody is here", which is a social-proof problem, not a signal (row 4).
- Two founders in dialogue as a concept this round: the mechanism is real and the format is absent from the sample, but it needs the filmed founder's written consent and footage that does not exist yet (row 5).
- "Experience required" as a literal line: it reads as gatekeeping and excludes the beginner the brief names; the non-literal inverse carries the mechanism without the insult (row 3). The critic also failed "If you already run something, this is for you" on all three of Harry Dry's tests in the interrogation review; the concepts carry the inverse with a situation instead.
