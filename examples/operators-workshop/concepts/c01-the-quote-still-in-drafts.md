## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: before any claim about AI lands, the viewer must feel "this is about my evening", not "this is another AI course". The ad shows the moment, then the room.
- Mirror at the trigger stage (`library/strategy/levinger-three-realities-teep.md`): show the situation before the product, because a problem-aware viewer recognizes the moment faster than the category.
- Problem-naming (`library/copy/hook-taxonomy.md`, problem-naming; `library/copy/angle-families.md`, problem-led): naming the problem more precisely than the viewer would name it signals that the writer has been there.
- Availability and vividness (`library/psychology/availability-and-vividness.md`): an object and an hour (a draft, 10pm) are easier to picture and recall than "admin" or "operations".
- Write to the edge (`library/psychology/dont-design-for-average.md`): the owner who writes every quote themselves is the edge; owners who batch admin on Sunday recognize themselves from it.
- Copy move: the hook names the object and the hour; the kicker puts AI and the room in the same frame (founders who run businesses, observed on the About page); the body shows the first step (see what is included on the About page; start the trial). No question about the viewer's personal attributes. Hook 5's cause (the blank page) is hypothetical: it comes from the founders' persona sketch, not from any customer.
