mechanism

## The one idea

A carousel that hands over the first step for free (find the one task you redo every week and write its steps down, shown on a made-up example) and invites the viewer to bring that list to Operators Workshop and build it there themselves.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: the viewer must feel that the stall was a missing task definition, not a missing talent, and leave the ad holding something they made.
- Hook, keep, reward (`library/copy/hormozi-callout-value-cta.md`): the value section is usable without buying anything; the reward is the written list itself.
- The generation effect (`library/psychology/shotton-illusion-of-choice.md`): people remember what they produce themselves; writing their own steps is generation, reading a curriculum is not.
- The IKEA effect (`library/psychology/endowment-and-ikea-effect.md`): a small early effort raises how much the result is valued; the list is theirs before they join.
- Four forces (`library/psychology/jtbd-four-forces.md`): p6's anxiety is that it did not stick last time because of them (persona file, inferred). A first step on paper, away from the chat tab, is small enough to lower that anxiety without promising anything.
- Make the invisible visible (`library/psychology/make-the-invisible-visible.md`): the repeated task is invisible until it is written as steps; the carousel's made-up example shows what "written as steps" looks like, then the viewer does their own.
- Mechanism-led (`library/copy/angle-families.md`, mechanism): the cards explain why the first step is a list and not a tool, and stop there.
- Copy move: card 1 names the object (the email in the sent folder) beside the chat tab, so the frame says AI; cards 1 to 3 carry one made-up example, labeled as made up; card 4 gives the paste-and-check step; card 5 says the viewer builds it. Nothing promises that anyone will look at, fix or build the viewer's task, and nothing implies a busy room.

6. (`curiosity-gap`) "Step one does not happen in the chat tab. It happens in the sent folder."
