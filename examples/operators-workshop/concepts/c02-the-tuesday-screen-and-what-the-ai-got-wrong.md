## The one idea

Instead of a count or a revenue line, show one real, dated screen from the founders' own business with the part the AI got wrong marked, and what they changed.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: before any claim lands, the viewer must feel "these people use it on real work, and they check it". A solution-aware owner's fear is not that AI cannot draft; it is that a draft with an error in it reaches a customer (p1 status game, inferred).
- Make the invisible visible (`library/psychology/make-the-invisible-visible.md`): a real screen shows the work; the labour illusion (Buell and Norton 2011) suggests that process seen is valued above a result stated.
- Pratfall (`library/psychology/shotton-choice-factory.md`): an admitted flaw can raise trust when it is real; a staged one reads as the trick it is.
- Costly signaling (`library/psychology/costly-signaling.md`): circling the AI's mistake is a cost a seller with nothing real to show would not pay.
- Satisficing (`library/psychology/satisficing.md`): the marked line and the change remove the catastrophe (a wrong draft goes out) before any benefit is offered.
- Make it precise (`library/psychology/shotton-illusion-of-choice.md`): one date and one line are easier to believe than a round claim.
- Copy move: the screen is the image and the circle is the hook. The kicker says whose screen it is and what Operators Workshop is. The body says what the AI got wrong, what changed, and what the screen does not prove. Proof-led per `library/copy/angle-families.md`. No question about the viewer's personal attributes.

## Six hooks

1. (`confession-or-pratfall`) "The AI drafted this. The circled line is what it got wrong."
2. (`question`) "Which line in this AI draft would you have fixed?"
3. (`comparison`) "The AI's draft vs the version that went out, side by side, same date."
4. (`demonstration`) "Watch the circled line, then the changed version, on a dated screen from one of the founders' businesses."
5. (`specific-number`) "One real screen. One date. One line the AI got wrong."
6. (`pov`) "POV: one of the founders opens the AI's draft and circles the line it got wrong."

Hook 3 says "went out", which is true only if the catalog row says the changed version was sent; otherwise it is cut. Hook 6 runs only if the catalog row records which founder made the screen.
