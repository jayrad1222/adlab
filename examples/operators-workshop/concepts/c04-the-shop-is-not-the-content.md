## The one idea

For a seller, the morning is messages, returns and restock, not content; two of the founders sell on Etsy, Amazon, Shopify and TikTok, and the community is for running the shop, not only for making videos.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: the seller must feel "this is about my morning, not another AI video course" before any claim about AI lands.
- Problem-naming and problem-led (`library/copy/hook-taxonomy.md`, problem-naming; `library/copy/angle-families.md`, problem-led): name the morning more precisely than the seller would.
- Mirror at the trigger stage (`library/strategy/levinger-three-realities-teep.md`): the seller recognizes the inbox before any product.
- Context changes value (`library/psychology/context-changes-value.md`): a category swap. The rows read that address shop or brand owners (2 advertisers) frame AI as a content tool; framing it at the inbox and the restock decision changes what the product is taken to be.
- Availability and vividness (`library/psychology/availability-and-vividness.md`): a return, a restock call and last night's message are objects; "operations" is not.
- Four forces (`library/psychology/jtbd-four-forces.md`): the hook names the push; the body supplies the pull in the page's words.
- People like us (`library/psychology/godin-people-like-us.md`): two of the founders sell on the same four marketplaces.
- Copy move: the hook names objects from the seller's morning; the same frame names Operators Workshop as an AI community and carries the "not only for making videos" contrast; the statics add that two of the founders sell on the same platforms (owner-provided) and quote the tactics line (observed). No line about the founders' own mornings, which are unknown. No question about the viewer's personal attributes.
