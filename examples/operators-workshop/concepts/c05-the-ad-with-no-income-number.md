## The one idea

In a feed full of first-client and side-income lines, an ad that refuses to name an income number and shows instead what the founders actually share.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: before anything else, the skeptic must feel "this is not another income funnel". Until that fear is gone, no benefit is heard (p4 anxiety, inferred).
- Satisficing (`library/psychology/satisficing.md`): remove the fear of being funneled before offering anything.
- Costly signaling (`library/psychology/costly-signaling.md`): refusing the loudest lever in the category is a cost a course seller would not pay.
- Labels and the accusation audit (`library/sales/voss-toolkit.md`): naming the thing the viewer expects to see (a day counter to a first client, a price-going-up card) before they think it is the hook that selects the skeptic.
- Status and identity (`library/psychology/status-and-identity.md`): p4's identity is "does not get fooled twice" (inferred); the ad treats them as a peer, not a mark.
- Ugly and native (`library/strategy/hott-ugly-ads.md`): a typed note does not look like an ad, and the founder note is absent from the sample (market map section 8).
- Copy move: the hook points at a convention the sample shows (a day-counter guarantee card, a dollars-a-month headline, a price-going-up card), described in general terms, that this ad does not contain. The frame says what Operators Workshop is and who runs it, and names one concrete thing shared, in the About page's words: "the prompts, systems, wins and mistakes behind the work". No competitor is named, quoted or shown. No "we will not tell you" slogan as the headline: the critic failed it on visualize.

## Six hooks

1. (`pattern-interrupt`) "No day counter to a first client. No price-going-up card. A typed note from Jared."
2. (`question`) "Seen the card that counts down the days to a first client? This note has no counter."
3. (`specific-number`) "One dollar figure in this note: $97 a month."
4. (`comparison`) "A day counter to a first client vs the founders' own builds, mistakes left in."
5. (`negative-or-warning`) "Before the next price-going-up card, read one note with no deadline in it."
6. (`identification`) "For the builder who scrolls past every dollars-a-month headline."

Hook 4 rests on builds being shared as they happen today, and hook 5 on the About page losing "even after it rises to $129"; both wait on those founder decisions.

## The CTA

Button: `Learn more`. A solution-aware viewer is not ready for `Sign up`. Self-selection pattern (`library/copy/cta-patterns.md`): "This is not an income plan. The About page lists what gets shared: 'the prompts, systems, wins and mistakes behind the work.' 7 days free, then $97 a month, cancel anytime."

## Landing continuation

The click goes to the About page (`business/site/about/landing/page.txt`). Its first line is "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less." For this concept that line is not a weak match; it contradicts the ad's one point. A viewer who clicked an ad with no income talk lands on "earn more". Continuity is broken, and the concept cannot be `ready` with a broken continuation (`library/rubrics/concept-brief-schema.md`). c05 stays blocked until the About page tagline no longer carries "earn more". Two more lines in the interrogation's "Before any test runs" conflict with it: "even after it rises to $129" is a deadline on the page a no-deadline note sends people to (it contradicts hook 5 and p4's "Nothing is going up"), and "Get direct answers and support from us" is close to the support promises the standing brief rules out. The idea does continue further down: "Our builds as they happen: the prompts, systems, wins and mistakes behind the work." The founders decide all three edits.

## Policy and truth risk flags

Checked against `library/platform/meta-policy-pitfalls.md`, `library/platform/ftc-endorsements.md` and `library/platform/creator-category-truth-rules.md`.

- Income or results: no figure but the price and no promise. Hooks 3 and 6 use money words ("$97", "dollar"), and automated review may still read money words in a business-opportunity category. If a hook is rejected, the first body line of the note or of the plain-text card becomes the hook and the money words move to the body.
- Landing: "earn more" is an income implication on the page the ad sends people to: `landing-mismatch`, and for this concept a broken continuation and a block (above).
- Urgency: none in the ad; hook 5 says the note has no deadline, which is false in spirit while the page says "even after it rises to $129". Gated on that edit.
- Competitors: none named, quoted or shown. "Day counter to a first client", "dollars-a-month headline" and "price-going-up card" describe conventions in general terms and must stay general; no real ad appears, blurred or not.
- Founder note (`library/platform/ftc-endorsements.md`): Jared speaks as the founder, so no material connection needs a separate disclosure; every line must be true and approved by him; no customer story, no first-person customer line.
- Personal attributes: hook 6 describes what the viewer does in the feed, not their finances; hook 2 asks what they have seen; no line says or implies the viewer has failed or lacks money. None identified.
- Support: the note promises no answers, reviews or response times (standing brief); it does not echo "Get direct answers and support from us".
- Revenue figures: the "$1.5M" line is excluded (no catalog row; the critic blocked it as an ad arm in the interrogation review).
