## The one idea

Name what the founders do not deliver (no done-for-you, no guarantees, no build reviews, no response-time promises) so the builder who wants to do the work picks it, and the one who wants it done picks something else.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: the builder must feel "this one will not promise me something and then fail to deliver it" before any reason to join can land.
- Remove the bad outcome first (`library/psychology/satisficing.md`): p4's fear is another funnel in disguise (persona file, anxiety, inferred); the "no" list removes it before the pitch.
- Say the doubt before the viewer does (`library/sales/voss-toolkit.md`, accusation audit): this is the hook family that selects the skeptic, the viewer most other ads lose.
- Red sneakers (`library/psychology/shotton-illusion-of-choice.md`): an empty space where 5 of 12 advertisers put a guarantee can read as confidence, because p4 has seen the guarantees (persona file, sophistication, inferred).
- A cost a funnel would not pay (`library/psychology/costly-signaling.md`): turning buyers away in the ad is a cost a volume funnel would not take, which is what makes it credible.
- Exclusion as status (`library/psychology/status-and-identity.md`) and writing to the edge (`library/psychology/dont-design-for-average.md`): a community that says what it will not deliver makes joining a choice the builder makes, not a deal they fall for.
- Copy move: the hook is one picture (an empty badge corner, a kitchen table, a closed build); the four items sit in the body, scoped to the founders and in the standing brief's words, because the critic failed the list as a headline on the visualize test; the frame says "AI community" so a stranger can place it; then one line of what is there, in the About page's words and gated on being true today; then the self-selection CTA (`library/copy/cta-patterns.md`).

## Landing continuation

The click goes to the About page (`business/site/about/landing/page.txt`). "Our builds as they happen" and "Share your progress and build alongside other members." are on it, word for word. The conflict is direct: the ad says no reply-time promise and no build reviews from the founders, and the page says "Get direct answers and support from us inside the community." and "If you’re new to AI, we’re here to assist you!" A viewer who clicked because of the "no" list meets a support line that reads like a promise. Continuity is weak and close to broken. The same page carries "earn more", an income implication this skeptic is inferred to discount on sight, and "even after it rises to $129" (an urgency line). Gate: the concept cannot run until the founders' decisions on "earn more", the $129 line and "Get direct answers and support from us" have shipped to the page (interrogation, "Before any test runs"). If the founders keep the support line unchanged, continuity is `broken` and this concept is blocked.
