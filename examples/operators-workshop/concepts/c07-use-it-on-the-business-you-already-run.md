## The one idea

The AI ads in this feed sell a route to income from other people's businesses or audiences; this one is about using AI on the business you already run, from founders who run theirs.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: before any claim lands, the owner must feel "this is not a pitch to become an AI freelancer". A side-by-side of two uses of AI makes the category legible in one glance.
- Name the alternative, then choose the category (`library/strategy/dunford-positioning.md`): placing "sell AI to other businesses" beside "use it on your own" moves the judging frame from "another AI course" to "something for my operation".
- Context sets value in the first two seconds (`library/psychology/context-changes-value.md`): a pitch deck for a stranger's shop and the owner's own drafts folder put AI in two different places before a word of the body is read.
- Write to the edge (`library/psychology/dont-design-for-average.md`): the owner with a running business is the edge; the client-seeker reads the left column as theirs and moves on, which is the point.
- Keep it concrete (`library/psychology/shotton-illusion-of-choice.md`, keep it concrete; `library/copy/harry-dry-three-tests.md`): every row of the table is an object (a pitch deck, a cold message, a supplier quote, an invoice folder), not a quality. The critic failed the abstract "If you already run something, this is for you" on all three tests; the objects carry that inverse instead.
- Comparison as the fair accounting (`library/copy/angle-families.md`, comparison): the alternative is stated as a business model, fairly, and no advertiser is named. Evaluation-stage format (`library/strategy/levinger-three-realities-teep.md`: comparison tables at evaluation).
- Copy move: the hook names two places AI can go; the table lists objects, and the right column is written as the viewer's own ("Your ..."), not as what the community covers; the body says who runs the room, in the About page's own words; the CTA restates the fit condition. Nothing about earnings, clients won or time saved.
