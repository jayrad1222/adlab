## Where the sample is thin for every persona

- No transcripts: all 156 rows are still-reviewed; nothing here rests on what a video says aloud.
- No customer language: every language bank is below the schema minimum of ten verbatim customer phrases and holds owner language and market language only, labeled. Where to look is listed in each file: reviews of competitor communities on Skool discovery pages, Reddit threads about AI courses and about the persona's trade, comments under the cited ads once captured, and the founders' own customer messages with consent.
- No interviews: the five customers in the interrogation are the founders' hypotheses. Every trigger moment, workaround and objection table is hypothetical or market-voiced until five intake calls exist.
- The three open personas (p1, p2, p3) are the founders' own operating situations. That is the opportunity and the risk: the evidence that these buyers exist in volume is the founders' experience, not the sample.
