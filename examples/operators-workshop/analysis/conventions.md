# Conventions worth inverting: Operators Workshop market

From the market map. Each convention names the advertisers running it; the inverse is a candidate for the opposite pass in `/adlab:angles`, where it gets a mechanism, a persona and a cheapest test or is rejected.

| # | Convention | Who runs it | Inverse to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crossed-out price down to free or $9 | AI Operator CLUB, AI Video Bootcamp, Kurs, The Claude Club, Arcads discount cards | Name the price with the reason it is right; no strikethrough; "cheap is not the point" |
| 2 | Income implication (first client in 90 days, $3K a month, side income) | Maker School, AI Operator CLUB, AI Video Bootcamp, Hashtag lesson 4 | "We will not tell you what you will earn. Here is what we did last Tuesday." |
| 3 | "No experience needed" | 9 of 12 advertisers | "If you already run something, this is for you"; selecting on experience of running, not of code |
| 4 | Member-count or team-count badge | AI Video Bootcamp, Arcads, Skool co-brands | A small number stated plainly ("5 members as of today") or a dated artifact instead of a count |
| 5 | Founder-to-camera selfie with word-by-word captions | 6 of 12 | Founder note (typed letter), or two founders in dialogue, or the founder's hands at the real screen with no face |
| 6 | "The ad is the demo" of AI realism | AI Video Bootcamp, Arcads | The ad is the demo of a boring, real task done faster, with the check shown |
| 7 | Lapsed-checkout urgency ("price going up") | AI Automation Society (one advertiser: 4201948366761992, 1750739099623552; corrected 2026-10-23, no Maker School ad in the sample shows a price rise) | "Nothing is going up; here is what you would have seen in week one" |
| 8 | Free lesson list as the whole offer | Hashtag NoCode, The Claude Club | A paid first week with a visible first result and a refund you do not have to ask for |
