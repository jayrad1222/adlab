# AI Video Bootcamp

Sample: 15 of a reported 837 active ads (capped). Still-reviewed.

**Angles.** "The ad is the demo": fashion and lifestyle montages labeled "This is AI in 2026" or "100% AI generated" (3366900353513536, 2294005904669622, 1894923588153698); price anchoring against studio shoots and agencies ($5,000 shoot versus one laptop, 1587585239519935; agency $15,000 versus $9, 1801930964690191); a contrarian reframe "tools are not the skill, directing is" in police-skit videos (1611195850348762, 1994721787907520); offer cards with $9/month, $55/year, strikethrough and "save 50%" (1403311474997024, 1920683551935476); a comment-reaction opener that turns skepticism into a demo (28188256047504517).

**Dominant proof.** Demonstration of realism (the output itself); unsourced counts ("25,000+ members", "21,100+ creators") and income lines ("$3K/month", "first paid deal in 3 weeks on average").

**Price signal.** Charm $9/month, per-day $0.30, anchored against $5,000 and $15,000. Says cheap and high-volume.

**Distinctive assets.** The "This is AI" label; the skit characters; the yearly-tier card.

**Oldest.** 1506202640947121 (145 days, listicle with the $1,500 to $3,000 arithmetic), 1403311474997024 (108 days, tier card). Clue, not verdict.

**Adapt.** The two-tier card with a real guarantee carried across tiers (1403311474997024); the comment-reaction opener on a real member question (28188256047504517); the comparison hook that names what is being replaced (1587585239519935).

**Avoid.** Income claims and member counts without a source; the mismatch between fashion visuals and small-business copy (2294005904669622, 1429775445162176).
