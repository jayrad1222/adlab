# Business brief: Operators Workshop

Example run. The site is the Skool About page (188 words); there is no separate marketing site. Observed lines come from that page on 2026-10-23.

## What it is (observed)

A private Skool community, "By Jared Ritsko", priced at $97/month with a 7-day free trial, price locked for members "even after it rises to $129", cancel anytime. The page shows 5 members at capture. Tagline: "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less." Founders named on the page: Jared, Julianna and Daniel, who "run real businesses doing over $1.5M a year in combined sales." Three included elements: builds as they happen ("the prompts, systems, wins and mistakes"), a Build with AI track ("tools, websites, content and automations"), and an AI + Business Tactics track ("find and keep customers, simplify operations and understand your numbers"). A "Build with us" section promises direct answers and support inside the community.

## What the founders say it is (owner-provided)

A community where the founders share what they are testing in their own businesses, what worked and what did not, and members compare notes. Jared and Julianna built an ecommerce brand (Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, TikTok); Daniel runs an operations software company and a custom fabrication business. The standing brief rules out advertising guided implementation, done-for-you work, guaranteed outcomes, individual build reviews, unlimited support or response-time promises. The audience includes curious beginners who do not own a business, aspiring builders and service providers, and existing owners. No numeric before-and-after claims have been verified. $97/month is a price hypothesis.
