## Landing continuation

Gate: nothing runs until the founders' decisions on "earn more", "even after it rises to $129" and "Get direct answers and support from us" have shipped to the About page (interrogation, "Before any test runs"). If the founders keep the support line unchanged, continuity is broken and this concept is blocked. The first screen must then repeat what the founders do not deliver, what they post, and the trial and price. Today's About page: weak and close to broken. "Our builds as they happen" is on it word for word, but nothing says what is not offered, and "Get direct answers and support from us inside the community." and "If you’re new to AI, we’re here to assist you!" read as the support promise the ad refuses (R5). Also "earn more" (income implication) and "even after it rises to $129" (urgency).
