## Landing continuation

Gate: nothing runs until the founders' decisions on "earn more", "even after it rises to $129" and "Get direct answers and support from us" have shipped to the About page (interrogation, "Before any test runs"). The first screen must then repeat the owner's own business as the place AI goes, and the price and terms (7 days free, then $97 a month, cancel anytime). Today's About page: weak, not broken. Price and terms match; the line that breaks it is the first line, "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less.", which names neither the owner nor the comparison and carries an income implication ("earn more"). The line closest to the right column, "AI + Business Tactics: find and keep customers, simplify operations and understand your numbers.", sits third. The category is the founders' call (R3).

## Why it should work

Comparison angle that names the alternative and then picks the category (library/strategy/dunford-positioning.md; library/psychology/context-changes-value.md): a stranger's pitch deck beside the owner's own drafts folder makes the category legible before the body is read. It answers p1's trust cue, "being sold a course by someone with no real business" (owner-provided paraphrase, interrogation answer 4), with the About page's "We run real businesses", and the right column stays the viewer's own objects, with no claim the community covers them (critic note 6(b)). Evidence: About page and standing brief per the v2 ledger; market map sections 4 and 5. Label: inferred mechanism; hypothetical effect.

## Formats

A solution-aware owner (p1) is at evaluation, so the lead is a lightly polished us-versus-them table that makes the category legible in one glance, with a native text-only card beside it as the cheapest test of the line itself. The video is native and hands-only (a founder's own screen, no face, no mock-ups), because the proof this concept can show is a founder working on their own business (R7: hands at the real screen), and it stays held until that screen is recorded. No carousel (the idea is one comparison, not a sequence) and no UGC (a creator cannot truthfully say they run the founders' businesses).
