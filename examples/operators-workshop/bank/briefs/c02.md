## Landing continuation

The first screen must repeat the mistake and who runs the community. Today's About page opens on "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less." It does not repeat the screen or the mistake; the idea continues in the included list ("the prompts, systems, wins and mistakes behind the work. We've made expensive mistakes."), so continuity is weak, not broken. The line that weakens it is "earn more" (an income implication; landing-mismatch). The CTA names only what the page shows (who runs it, the two tracks, the price), not proof the page does not carry. Gate: nothing in this concept runs until the About page edits named in interrogation v2 ("Before any test runs") ship: "earn more", "even after it rises to $129" and "Get direct answers and support from us"; and until the founders confirm their businesses are run today and builds are shared as they happen.
