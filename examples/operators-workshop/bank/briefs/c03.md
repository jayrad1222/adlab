## Landing continuation

The first screen must repeat who runs it, ideally the three kinds of business. Today's About page opens on "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less." The second paragraph repeats the 'who' ("We're Jared, Julianna and Daniel. We run real businesses") but names no trade and no shop, so continuity is weak, not broken. The line that weakens it is "earn more" (an income implication; landing-mismatch). A line naming the three businesses would make it strong, if that founder agrees. Gate: nothing in this concept runs until the About page edits named in interrogation v2 ("Before any test runs") ship: "earn more", "even after it rises to $129" and "Get direct answers and support from us"; and until the founders confirm their businesses are run today and builds are shared as they happen. Lines naming the fabrication shop also wait on the named founder's OK to be named with their business type.
