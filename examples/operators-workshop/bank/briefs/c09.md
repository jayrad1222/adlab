## Landing continuation

Gate: nothing runs until the founders' decisions on "earn more", "even after it rises to $129" and "Get direct answers and support from us" have shipped to the About page (interrogation, "Before any test runs"). The first screen must then repeat that the member builds the task themselves, plus the trial and price. Today's About page: weak, not broken. "Our builds as they happen" is on it under WHAT'S INCLUDED; the first line, "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less.", does not mention a task. The line that breaks it is "Get direct answers and support from us inside the community.", which a viewer holding a list may read as the build-review promise the standing brief rules out. Also "earn more" (income implication) and "even after it rises to $129" (urgency).

## Why it should work

Hook, keep, reward (library/copy/hormozi-callout-value-cta.md) with the generation and IKEA effects (library/psychology/shotton-illusion-of-choice.md; library/psychology/endowment-and-ikea-effect.md): one worked example, labeled made up, shows the step, and the viewer leaves holding a list they made. It answers p6's market-voiced stall, "keep watching tutorials but still do everything manually" (ad 2032976233999151, persona file), with a first step on paper rather than another tool, lowering the four-forces anxiety (library/psychology/jtbd-four-forces.md). Evidence: About page and standing brief per the v2 ledger; market map sections 3 and 5. Label: inferred mechanism; hypothetical effect.
