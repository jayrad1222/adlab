## Landing continuation

The first screen must repeat the seller, the operations and AI, ideally the four marketplaces. Today's About page opens on "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less." It names no marketplace and no seller task; "simplify operations" appears only in the third included item, so continuity is weak, not broken. The line that weakens it is "earn more" (an income implication; landing-mismatch). A line naming the four marketplaces would make it strong. For the POV cut (no founder credential), the body is: "Operators Workshop is an AI community about running a business. The About page's tactics line includes 'simplify operations and understand your numbers.' 7 days free, then $97 a month." Gate: nothing in this concept runs until the About page edits named in interrogation v2 ("Before any test runs") ship: "earn more", "even after it rises to $129" and "Get direct answers and support from us"; and until the founders confirm builds are shared as they happen today.
