## Landing continuation

The first screen must repeat the owner and the unfinished quote, or at least the admin after the paid work, and say AI. Today's About page does not: its first line is "Learn to use AI to buy back your time, earn more and spend less." Continuity is weak, not broken; the second paragraph carries the 'who' ("We run real businesses"). The line that weakens it is "earn more", an income implication (landing-mismatch). The page has no 'who it is not for' section, so the CTA points only to what is included. Gate: nothing in this concept runs until the About page edits named in interrogation v2 ("Before any test runs") ship: "earn more", "even after it rises to $129" and "Get direct answers and support from us"; and until the founders confirm builds are shared as they happen today.
