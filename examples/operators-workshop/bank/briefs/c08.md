## Why it should work

The price is read as information (library/psychology/price-is-information.md) and the first number sets the frame (library/psychology/anchoring-and-framing.md): one $97 with nothing struck through, tied to the founders' own AI builds, moves p6's inferred comparison (the chat-tool subscription they already pay; persona file, objections, price, hypothesis) from a tool to someone's working builds; the price-stated CTA (library/copy/cta-patterns.md) removes the hidden-price fear. Evidence: About page lines in the v2 ledger; market map sections 3 and 7; competitor offer cards in competitor_refs. Label: inferred mechanism; hypothetical effect.
