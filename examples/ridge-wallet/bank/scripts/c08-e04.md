## UGC brief

**Persona and situation.** The creator plays a giver (a reenactment of a situation, labeled on screen; never presented as a customer or as someone who bought one). The moment: a few weeks before a gift date, three Ridge wallets on a kitchen table, buying for a man who says he needs nothing. Situation from `personas/p5-two-weeks-out-and-he-says-he-needs-nothing.md`.

**The single angle.** Problem-led: for a hard-to-buy-for man, three named Ridge wallet finishes make the choice, and a wrong guess can go back in usable condition within 99 days of the order, as long as it is not engraved.

**Three hook options.**
1. Question: "Which Ridge wallet: Royal Black, Alpine Navy or Matte Olive? Guess wrong, and exchange it within 99 days of your order."
2. Callout: "For someone who says he needs nothing: a Ridge wallet in Royal Black, Alpine Navy or Matte Olive, and 99 days from your order to exchange it."
3. Negative or warning: "Before you engrave the wallet: a personalized Ridge cannot be returned. A plain one can, within 99 days of your order."

**The body beats.**
- Problem: say the situation as a situation ("buying for someone who says he needs nothing"), not as your own story.
- Mechanism: hold up the three finishes by name; say the window counts from the order, not from the day he opens it.
- Proof: show the Returns & Exchanges page on your phone: the 99-day line, the usable-condition line, the exchange-shipping line, and the line that personalized items are not eligible for returns. Read them; do not add to them.
- First step: "Pick one of the three. Keep the order email."

**On-screen text plan.** 0s: "Paid partnership. Reenactment." at the top of the safe zone, the hook in the middle band. Then "99 days from your order", "In usable condition", "Free U.S. exchange once approved", "No engraving", and the end card "Pick one of the three. Return terms as of [recording date]; they can change." Each under eight words except the hook and the end card.

**The CTA.** Default path with the reversal inside it: "Pick one of the three. If he wants another finish, return it in usable condition within 99 days of your order; once the return is approved, the exchange ships free in the U.S." Destination: the landing Ridge builds for interrogation R5 (the three finishes and the exchange line on the first screen). The ad does not run before that page exists.

**What not to say.**
- No "I bought this", "my husband", "my dad", or any line that implies you gave one; you are playing the situation.
- No delivery date, no "in time", no "two weeks".
- No price, no sale, no code, no "perfect gift", no "he will love it".
- Never say he can start the exchange; the order email is the buyer's.
- Never say an engraved wallet cannot be exchanged; the page says only that personalized items cannot be returned.
- Never say "free exchange" without "once the return is approved" and "usable condition".
- Nothing about other countries' returns (international customers pay for a tracked label).

**Ugly is fine.** Phone on a stack of books, kitchen light, the wallets on whatever table you have. Captions and clear speech are the only quality bars.

**Shot list.**
1. You at the table, three wallets fanned in one hand, hook spoken in the first second. 3s.
2. The wallets set down one at a time, each name said. 4s.
3. Your phone showing the Returns & Exchanges page, the 99-day line. 3s.
4. The same page, the usable-condition and exchange-shipping lines. 3s.
5. The same page, "Personalized items ... aren't eligible for returns or refunds". 3s.
6. One wallet picked up, the other two pushed aside. 2s.
7. A printed order email, order number area pointed at, all personal data blank. 2s.
8. End card. 3s.

**Deliverables and aspect ratios.** Three raw takes (one per hook), 9:16, 15 to 45 seconds each, plus the shots as separate clips; a 4:5 crop of the best take; caption file. "Paid partnership" and "Reenactment" on screen from the first frame.

**Rights and disclosure.** Twelve months, Meta and TikTok, Ridge may cut and caption, disclosure stays on screen and in the caption. Written consent and the usage agreement signed before filming. The three wallets are supplied by Ridge; if the creator keeps them, that is a material connection and the disclosure says so.
