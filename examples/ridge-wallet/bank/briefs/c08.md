## Lines cut

- "Three finishes. 99 days to exchange.": Version-1 headline: an exchange line with no room for 'usable condition' and 'after your return is approved' (critic item 6).
- "99 days from the order to exchange": Same: exchange line without its conditions.
- "Free U.S. exchange shipping": Version-1 description: leaves out 'after your return is approved'.
- "He says he needs nothing. Royal Black, Alpine Navy or Matte Olive, and 99 days from your order to exchange it.": Version-1 short text: the exchange conditions do not fit under 125 characters; the short text now states no exchange term.
- "The gift he will actually use.": Reaction and continued use are hypothetical in the brief's ledger.
- "Arrives in time for the holidays.": Shipping page not captured; no delivery promise.
- "He can swap it himself.": No captured page says a gift recipient can start a return or exchange.
- "The perfect gift for the man who has everything.": Superlative, unfalsifiable, any brand could run it; on the brief's never list.
