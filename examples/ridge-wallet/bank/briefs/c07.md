## Lines cut

- "One owner's wallet, not a promise about yours.": The 'results not typical' move that library/platform/ftc-endorsements.md says no longer does the job (critic item 2); replaced by lines that stay on this wallet.
- "This is one owner's wallet after [N] years. It is not a promise about any other.": Same disclaimer in the long copy; cut for the same reason.
- "See the wallet and its order date, then decide.": Promises what the landing does not show (critic item 7).
- "Built for life, and here is the proof.": Generalizes from one wallet to a lifespan; banned as a headline by the brief.
- "Still covered by the warranty.": False for scratches and for wallets bought before 14 October 2025 (Warranty Policy page); no warranty line on this ad.
- "Metal ages better than leather.": Unfalsifiable, generalizing and an implied comparison.
- "The plates scratch. The cards stay.": Version-1 headline: 'the cards stay' reads as a rule for every Ridge; replaced by a line about this owner's wallet.
