The first screen must repeat the return started within 99 days of the order, its exclusions, and the three beats (order, move the cards over, decide). Today's landing, the Ridge Wallet 2.0 product page (Classic, Royal Black) captured 2026-10-23, is weak: the trial appears only in the rotating top bar as "99-DAY RISK-FREE TRIAL" and as "Free shipping + returns" under the button; the terms the ad states live on the Returns & Exchanges page in the footer, and the first screen leads with "FRIENDS & FAMILY SALE | UP TO 40% OFF" and the review count. "RISK-FREE" sits beside conditional terms, and "Personalize This Item" carries no return warning. The run lands only on a standard finish. Three beats under the button would make it strong; that edit is Ridge's.
