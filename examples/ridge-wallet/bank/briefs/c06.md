## Landing continuation

The first screen must repeat the three terms with the review condition: a lost or stolen aluminum Ridge replaced for $17.95 up to two times, a defective part replaced free, scratches not covered, claims reviewed and deniable, terms dated. Today's landing (Ridge Wallet 2.0 aluminum product page) is weak: the spec line "Built for Life Warranty" sits below the button but the terms are one link away (the block's "LEARN MORE" link; that it opens the Warranty Policy page is inferred, the target was not captured), and the line that breaks fit is "backed by our new industry-leading Built for Life Warranty", an unsourced superlative the ad does not repeat; "No matter what comes your way, Ridge has your back" is broader than the terms. The fold shows the struck $95, $76 and the review count, none of which the ad mentions.

## Lines cut

- "Defective part: free. Lost aluminum Ridge: $17.95 to replace, up to twice. Scratches: not covered. Terms as of 23 Oct 2026.": Version-1 short text: states warranty terms without the review condition, and the condition does not fit under 125 characters; the short text now states no term.
- "Lost Ridge? $17.95 to replace today": Headline states a warranty term with no room for the review condition (coordinator rule, critic item 2).
- "The full terms are on ridge.com. Read them before you buy.": Version-1 CTA: the landing's first screen shows no terms (critic 7a).
- "Lifetime means lifetime, with the fine print in large type.": Fails falsify; the terms cap replacements at two and exclude cosmetic damage.
- "Wear and tear is covered too.": The wallet table names no remedy for wear; the brief says nothing about wear.
- "No other wallet brand prints its fee.": Comparison with other brands' warranties is rejected by the brief; unverifiable.
- "Replaced in days.": No source for a time to replacement; hypothetical in the brief's ledger.
