## Landing continuation

The first screen must repeat the return started within 99 days of the order and the exclusions (standard finishes; not personalized, warranty-claim or certain limited-edition items). Today's landing, the Ridge Wallet 2.0 product page (Classic, Royal Black, no limited-edition tag in the capture; confirm it is returnable before launch) captured 2026-10-23, is weak: it leads with "FRIENDS & FAMILY SALE | UP TO 40% OFF", the review count and $95 struck to $76, which for this skeptic reads as a gadget's price signal; the trial shows only as "99-DAY RISK-FREE TRIAL" in the rotating top bar and "Free shipping + returns" under the button. "Personalize This Item" sits on the same page with no warning that it ends the return right. A returns line under the button naming the exclusions would make it strong; that edit is Ridge's.

## Why it should work

Defaults and the status quo (library/psychology/defaults-and-status-quo.md): the old wallet stays available, so buying is not a verdict on p3's judgement, and his fear of "looking like he bought a gadget off an ad" (persona p3, anxiety, inferred) loses its stakes at the purchase stage; a rule that undoes the purchase serves a satisficer (library/psychology/satisficing.md).
