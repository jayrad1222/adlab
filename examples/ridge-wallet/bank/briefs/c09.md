## Why it should work

Price is information (library/psychology/price-is-information.md) and anchoring and framing (library/psychology/anchoring-and-framing.md), with costly signaling (library/psychology/costly-signaling.md) and the power of 'because' (library/psychology/shotton-choice-factory.md): for p3, a deep discount confirms the gadget suspicion (personas/p3-the-man-who-thinks-it-is-a-gadget.md, emotional identity: 'not taken in by clever products'), so one price with its terms and the right to deny printed under it lets him check what the money buys. ## Lines cut

- "Lost it? $17.95 to replace, today": Version-1 headline: states a warranty term with no room for the review condition.
- "Defective part? Replaced free.": Same: warranty term without the review condition.
- "Terms as of [date]": Version-1 description; the brief v2 dates the terms to 23 October 2026.
- "Never on sale.": False at capture and unprovable for the future.
- "Was $110.": Former-price claim with no documentation; $110 for this variant is unverified.
- "Cheaper than the $5 wallet you will replace three times.": Echoes a reviewer (insight only) and an unsourced number.
- "The last wallet you will buy.": Unfalsifiable lifespan promise; full replacements are capped at two.
