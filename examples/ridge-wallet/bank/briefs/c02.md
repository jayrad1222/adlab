## Landing continuation

The first screen must repeat the count (up to 12 cards and IDs, the ID among them). Today's landing, the Ridge Wallet 2.0 product page captured 2026-10-23, is strong on the claim and weak on the persona: "Holds 1-12 Cards" sits directly under "ADD TO CART $76 USD" and the description repeats "up to 12 IDs and credit cards", but at phone size the first screen leads with the sale banner, the review count and $95 struck to $76, so the count is one scroll down, and nothing mentions an ID or a working day. The page also lists "Swappable Cash Strap or Money Clip" next to the count; the ad never pairs the two, and the page does not say both fit at the maximum.
