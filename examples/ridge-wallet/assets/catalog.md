# Asset catalog: Ridge (minimalist wallets)

Written 2026-10-23 by `/adlab:assets` per `library/rubrics/asset-catalog-schema.md`.

**No files were supplied for this example run.** Ridge is a stand-in member and nobody at Ridge supplied anything, so `assets/` holds no photographs, clips, screenshots, documents, quotes or numbers and the catalog has no rows. Under the schema's first rule, the catalog never lists an asset that is not on disk.

The pages captured from ridge.com (`business/site/`) are not member assets: they are observed public pages, used in the brief as the source of the trial terms, the warranty terms, the product spec and five featured reviews. They prove what Ridge's pages said on 2026-10-23. They are not files Ridge handed over, and their screenshots stay out of the repo.

What this means downstream:

- Every concept and bank entry draws its claims from Ridge's captured pages (observed) and nothing else. Every entry's `asset_refs` is `none`.
- The five featured reviews are customer language for insight only. A customer's words in an ad need that customer's written consent for advertising, recorded as a row here with consent `granted`; none exists.
- No entry may show a person, a wallet photograph presented as a customer's, a worn wallet, or a number beyond the captured spec and terms.
- Nothing synthetic stands in for a missing asset. A generated image of a wallet or a pocket would prove only "this is what we imagined" and is not used.
