# Dango Products

Sample: 15 ads; 11 are one offer, the SK1 wallet at $79 struck through to $39 and $19 with a public code presented as exclusive ("you've been selected", "not everyone gets this code"). Still-reviewed.

**Angles.** Offer-led almost entirely: the price ladder on the product, a six-second cut showing the card slide, orange block statics. Two mechanism ads stand apart: "LISTEN ON FULL VOLUME" (the slide's sound as the product, 1736591054090233) and "STAY SLIM" macro hardware shots. One non-wallet demo (a fire starter).

**Dominant proof.** None; demonstration in the slide cuts.

**Price signal.** Deep discount with fake exclusivity; says "always on sale", which undermines the machined-hardware identity the brand otherwise projects.

**Adapt.** The sound-on mechanism demo: metal wallets have a tactile signature that leather cannot show, and only Dango uses it. Price, product and code in the first second (legibility) is worth noting even if the discount is not.

**Avoid.** A public code presented as reserved; a ladder of struck prices; an unfilled catalog template in the copy (28157272277296138).
