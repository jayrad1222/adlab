# Conventions worth inverting: minimalist wallet market

| # | Convention | Who runs it | Inverse to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Struck-through price ladder with an "exclusive" public code | Dango, Thread | Price stated once with its reason; the 99-day trial as the only concession |
| 2 | Superlatives ("best wallet ever", "#1 bestselling") | Andar, Ekster | A specific, small, checkable claim ("eleven cards and the bills, nothing else") |
| 3 | Scarcity and limited editions | Andar, Ridge, Thread | Abundance: "we will make this finish for as long as you ask" |
| 4 | Creator unboxing | Andar, Thread, Ekster | The owner's own wallet after years, no box |
| 5 | Catalogue tiles with no persona | Ridge, Bellroy | A problem-first static naming the moment of switching |
| 6 | Celebrity or host authority | Ekster | A nurse, a mechanic, a pilot with their own carry |
| 7 | Finish drops and licences as identity | Ridge, Thread | Identity through use: who carries it and why, not what colour |
| 8 | Slim and RFID as the headline | 7 of 9 | The back pocket and the posture: what the object ends |
