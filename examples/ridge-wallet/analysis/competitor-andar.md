# Andar

Sample: 15 ads around the Denner 2 relaunch and the Lo wallet; three duplicate pairs. Still-reviewed; mostly creator footage.

**Angles.** A comment card as the hook ("OH MY GOD I have been coveting a small slim wallet that has a full bill compartment", 1520735273186403): the viewer's own words name the unmet need. Identification callouts: "made by the girls, for the girls" (1988858838459438), "a small wallet for those of us who still carry cash. And coins." (1428488879491057). A luxury comparison, "this wallet cost me hundreds of dollars", holding a logo wallet beside the Lo (1131011726095206). News of a sequel ("the best wallet ever just got better"), restock scarcity ("always coveted, rarely in stock"), unboxings that open every pocket.

**Dominant proof.** UGC and demonstration of capacity; unsourced "hundreds of thousands carrying one".

**Price signal.** Not shown; scarcity does the work.

**Distinctive assets.** Kitchen-table unboxings; the magnet and pull tab; the cash slot as a feature in a slim category.

**Adapt.** The comment-as-hook format with a real comment (consent recorded); the honest "still carry cash and coins" callout, which admits what slim wallets usually hide; the quality-per-dollar comparison against a logo wallet.

**Avoid.** Unverifiable scarcity ("down to our last few", "no confirmed restock date"); a caption that names the wrong product (2635860816867558).
