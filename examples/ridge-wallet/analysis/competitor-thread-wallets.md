# Thread Wallets (indirect)

Sample: 15 ads: a Diet Coke collaboration (creator car-selfie "what I got" videos and product demos, 1548003933342721, 1265713422174307), Target availability ads ("Thread at Target", "a dream shopping trip", school-ready accessories), and a "Final Few Sale, up to 75% off" card (1084877057196159, 113 days). Still-reviewed.

**Angles.** Status by association (a drinks brand), availability and trust (a mass retailer), and clearance.

**Dominant proof.** UGC walk-throughs; none otherwise.

**Price signal.** Up to 75% off with no prices; says cheap and fun.

**What it means for Ridge.** A different buyer (younger, fabric, licensed drops) with one transferable idea: the licensed collaboration as an identity signal, which Ridge already runs with team wallets and Batman. The retailer-availability angle is a reminder that "where can I see it" is a trust question some buyers have.

**Avoid.** A bag as the hero of a wallet ad; "up to" discounts covering an unstated subset.
