# The Ridge (the stand-in member, for comparison)

Sample: 10 ads from The Ridge plus one each from Ridge EU and Ridge UK. Still-reviewed. Included so the member can see their own current pattern against the market.

**What runs now.** Dynamic catalogue tiles ("Upgrade your wallet to Ridge", three variants, 35 days) with no persona, proof or price; a limited-edition Phantom finish talk-to-camera (1402989268694829); a five-in-one power bank demo (1081840734441981, 6 variants); NFL team wallets with a callout ("the wallet that football fans have been gatekeeping", 2211059562779279); "The golden standard" static; EU and UK catalogue copy that states the 99-day risk-free trial.

**Read.** The finishes and licences carry identity; the trial and warranty carry trust; nothing in the active set names the buyer's situation (the worn bifold, the fat back pocket, the cards that fall out) or shows the metal after years of use. The site's constant sale banners say "premium, always discounted" while the ads say nothing about price at all.

**Adapt from the market.** Bellroy's wear-as-proof format for metal; Andar's comment-as-hook and honest cash callout; Dango's sound-on mechanism; Ekster's two-pockets comparison.
