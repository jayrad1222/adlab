# Ekster

Sample: 15 ads, but 14 of them sell the Vacuum TravelPack (a compression bag co-designed with the podcast host Steven Bartlett), not wallets; the one wallet ad ties the Cosmic Orange smart wallet to the iPhone 17 colour (1562981438714928). Four copies of the same "me and my husband have the same carry-on, I fit 10 outfits" video run from different Pages and count as one piece of evidence. Still-reviewed.

**Angles.** Authority transfer (a known host), three-step demos (load, zip, pump), a household comparison (10 versus 5 outfits), a peer pack-with-me before-and-after (2594876510944721), certification badges plus "never check a bag again".

**Dominant proof.** Demonstration and authority; unsourced counts ("50,000+ 5-star reviews", "2M+ travelers", "1,578,884 interactions").

**Price signal.** Not shown; a 100-day trial and lifetime warranty on the end card.

**What it means for a wallet brand.** Ekster's active wallet advertising is thin right now; its wallet claims (trackable, RFID, trial, warranty) sit in the sample only as table stakes. The transferable mechanism is the visible comparison (two identical bags, different results), which maps to "two identical pockets, different wallets".

**Avoid.** Counts without a source; celebrity reliance the member cannot buy.
