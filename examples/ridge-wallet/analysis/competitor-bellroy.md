# Bellroy

Sample: 15 ads (own Page; one Taiwanese retailer ad excluded), captured 2026-10-23. Still-reviewed.

**Angles.** Durability made visible: "Day 1 | Day 1000" before-and-after of a worn leather wallet (1046004753909784, 773 days), "Wallets that last. And last. And last." with six variants (1497017115093052, 363 days), new-versus-worn pairs (912140034982191), "Quality x time = age becomes it" in handheld footage (2165783148149714). Organisation made visible in packed-open pouches (513444261432452). Travel and tech bags carry the rest.

**Dominant proof.** Demonstration of wear; a three-year warranty. No counts, no testimonials.

**Price signal.** Never shown in the ad; the landing carries it. Says "pay once, keep it".

**Distinctive assets.** The worn wallet itself; day counters; macro leather grain.

**Oldest.** Five ads at 773 days, all durability or organisation demos. A clue that they keep running durability, not a verdict.

**Adapt.** Wear as proof is the strongest mechanism in the category, and nobody has done it for metal: a Ridge after five years of pockets is a different and checkable image. The day counter is a format worth borrowing.

**Avoid.** Landing a wallet ad on a 30-item category page (1046004753909784).
