LUIS MINIMALIST WALLET

₱590

We thought of improving the Paolo Minimalist Wallet design (7+ years) to maintain the small form factor and make the cards more secure but easily accessible, and we came up with the Luis Minimalist Wallet. The left part can easily hold up to 10 cards and 15 bills as seen in the photos here but still maintain a small footprint that will allow you to still put it in your front pocket.

The personalization can be found inside on the lower right hand corner of the pocket. Available in our Full Side and Oily Pull-up Leather Choices which retains the thickness from the tannery, the same thickness used in shoes to maintain it’s durability.

Unfolded Length:  21.5 cm
Folded Length: 10.5 cm

Thickness (No Content): 0.8 cm
Thickness (8-10 cards + 12-15 bills): 2.0 cm

This item is not engraved with the Astrid Leather Logo. Only the personalization/engraving will be used if availed.
