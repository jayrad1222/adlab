Same idea. More room to work with.

The Denner 2 is created in collaboration with Daryl-Ann Denner, keeping the pull tab and The Tether wristlet that made the original work, and adding real capacity on top of it. Two push tab windows sit on the front and inside, so the card you're reaching for is never on the wrong side. A secure bifold design with a hidden magnet keeps everything closed without a bulky clasp.

Extra card slots and a dedicated cash slot bring it up to holding 1 to 14 cards and 1 to 10 bills, flexible enough to run thin on a light day or actually earn its keep when it's carrying everything. It's built the same way the original was, functional first, thin enough to still fit in a pocket.
