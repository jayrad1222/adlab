Built for the miles between plans.

The Luna Active is a lightweight nylon crescent bag that stays put against your side while you move, whether that’s a brisk morning walk or a quick class before work. Its curved shape sits close, so it doesn’t bounce or swing when you pick up the pace, and your hands stay free for a dog leash on a loop around the neighborhood or a coffee on the way to the trailhead. Afterward, a damp cloth with a little mild soap handles most messes from a sweaty session or a spilled smoothie.

Inside, two open pockets keep your phone and earbuds within reach, and a zippered pocket holds your cards and keys. There’s still room for a wallet, sunscreen, and a snack for later without it ever feeling packed. Every Luna Active comes with an adjustable canvas strap for wearing it across the body, and the paracord bundle adds a braided strap for a shorter, sportier shoulder carry. By the time the workout’s done and you’re meeting friends for lunch, it’s the same bag, just with one less snack inside.
