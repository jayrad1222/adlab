# Business brief: Ridge (wallets)

Example run. Ridge is a public brand used as the stand-in member business; nobody at Ridge was interviewed. Every line is observed from the public site or marked inferred.

## What it is (observed)

A direct-to-consumer brand whose home page sells wallets, rings, luggage, power banks, phone cases, wedding bands, chains, a tracker card and drinkware. Wallet line: "Slim, RFID-blocking, built to last for life." Three standing promises on the home page: a lifetime guarantee ("Built to endure. For life"), a 99-day risk-free trial ("Live with it. If it's not everything we promised, return it for a full refund"), and fast free shipping. Collections include licensed and finish-led drops (Batman, Vacuum Pack, Atomic Purple, Phantom, Burnt Damascus) and finishes "pulled straight from the newest iPhone lineup." Promotions at capture: a Friends & Family sale up to 40% off, wallets up to 49% off, buy two get one free on power banks. Category page titles include "5 reasons why women love the Ridge wallet" and wallet kits.

## Who it seems to be for (inferred, with the cue)

Men who carry too much in a back pocket and like gear that reads as engineered; the finish drops and licensed collections suggest a buyer who treats the wallet as a small identity object; the "women love" collection title suggests a gift and a second buyer. Cue: the material, finish and licensing language; the guarantee and trial as the main reassurance.

## The promise in their words (observed)

"Future proof. Designed now. Backed by a lifetime warranty." "Elevate your everyday." "Built to last for life."

## Proof on the site (observed)

The lifetime warranty and the 99-day trial as risk reversal; "Founder Spec is the evidence it lasts" (a heritage claim); no customer numbers or reviews captured on the pages read.

## Tone and visual world (observed)

Short, uppercase section labels, product-forward, promotion-heavy at capture; dark finishes and metal.

## Claims a competitor could also make word for word (inferred)

"Slim." "RFID-blocking." "Built to last." "Lifetime warranty." "Free shipping." "Risk-free trial." Most direct competitors in the roster make three or more of these.

## What is missing (inferred)

A single problem statement in the customer's words. The ritual of switching wallets (what you leave behind). A reason the metal is better than leather for this buyer. The sale banners anchor on discount more than on the object.

## Addendum: pages captured for the angles step (observed 2026-10-23)

The interrogation's next step asked for the Returns/Exchanges and Warranty Policy pages and a wallet product page; they were captured read-only with `tools/landing.py` on 2026-10-23 (`site/returns-exchanges/`, `site/warranty-policy/`, `site/product-aluminum-black/`). Quotes are verbatim.

**The 99-day trial (Returns & Exchanges page).** "you can return or exchange your purchase within 99 days." "Returns must be initiated through our returns portal within 99 days of your purchase" (the clock starts at purchase, not delivery). "Returns are free for U.S. customers with a digital prepaid USPS label." "Items should be in usable condition to qualify for a refund or exchange." "We'll ship exchanged items for free within the U.S. after your return is approved." Not eligible: "Personalized items, warranty claim orders and certain limited edition items." "Original shipping fees aren't refundable." International customers pay for a tracked return label.

**The lifetime warranty (Warranty Policy page, "Built for Life Warranty").** Covers each Ridge Wallet "purchased on or after October 14th, 2025 from Ridge official websites, Ridge official Amazon listings, or other Ridge authorized retail partners"; covers "defects in materials and workmanship, loss, theft, damage from normal wear and tear, and accidental damage." Wallet terms: parts replacement for defective parts, free; full replacement for loss and theft or accidental damage, processing fee "$17.95" for wallets under $150 and "$27.95" at $150 and over; "Customers may receive up to two full replacements." Not covered, among others: "Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality, including scratches, dents, and discoloration"; products bought before 14 October 2025 or from unauthorized sellers. "Processing fees, terms, and conditions are subject to change at the discretion of Ridge." Gift recipients can start a claim.

**The product page (Ridge Wallet 2.0, Aluminum, Royal Black).** List price $95, shown at $76 ("20% off") with "ADD TO CART $76 USD" at capture; this corrects the brief's $110 for this variant (the collections page lists aluminum wallets at $95 and $110 by line; finance.md keeps 110 as a placeholder until the member names the hero product). Spec lines: "Holds 1-12 Cards", "Swappable Cash Strap or Money Clip", "RFID-Blocking Plates", "Built for Life Warranty", "10% Lighter". "Free shipping + returns" beside the button. Personalization is offered ("Personalize This Item"), which makes that item ineligible for return.

**Reviews on the product page.** "Rated 4.8 out of 5 stars, Based on 52,847 reviews" (the widget's count at capture; how it aggregates variants is not stated). An "AI-generated" summary of "100 most recent customer reviews" says, among praise, "Some note difficulty accessing cards once inserted, and a few mention durability concerns with screws loosening over time." Five reviews loaded, all under the "Featured" sort (chosen by the page, so a selection, not a sample).
