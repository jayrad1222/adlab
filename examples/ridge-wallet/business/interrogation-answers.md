# Interrogation answers: Ridge (wallets)

Example run. Nobody at Ridge answered these; every answer is the intake **default**, kept so the later steps can show how defaults flow through and where they fall short. A member answers in chat during `/adlab:intake`.

## Batch one: the customer and the fear

1. **Who is the customer trying to look like after buying, and to whom?** default: someone who has their act together, to their partner and their peers. Inferred addition from the site: someone whose gear is engineered, to other gear-minded men.
2. **What does the customer do instead today, including nothing?** default: they muddle through with a cheaper tool; nothing costs them a fat pocket and a bit of embarrassment. Hypothesis.
3. **Which claim could a competitor make word for word?** default: most of the home page. Confirmed by the brief.
4. **Worst fear if it goes wrong?** default: they paid and nothing changed, and someone they told finds out. Inferred for this category: the wallet feels like a gadget, the cards fall out, the money clip scratches, the "lifetime" promise turns into a support ticket.
5. **What do you do that is inefficient on purpose?** default: nothing on purpose. Inferred: the lifetime warranty and the 99-day trial are expensive promises kept on purpose (costly signals).
6. **Ritual and first week?** default: they buy it, use it or forget it. Inferred: moving cards out of the old wallet is a ritual nobody on the site describes.

## Batch two: price, fit and proof

7. **What does your price say?** default: reasonable, a bit under the main competitor. Observed: $75 to $110 with frequent deep discounts, which says premium object, discount habit.
8. **Who is wrong for you?** default: people who want it done for them or want a guarantee; not applicable here. Inferred: people who carry cash and coins, or who want soft leather.
9. **What just happened when they buy?** default: something broke, a bill, or they saw someone else doing it. Inferred: a torn old wallet, a trip, a gift occasion.
10. **What would have to be true about you that competitors cannot say?** default: we care more. Inferred from the site: a long-running metal wallet with finish drops and licensed editions, and a public founder-era product as evidence of longevity.
11. **More expensive and better?** default: more support. Inferred: engraving, repair-for-life, a trade-in of the old wallet.
12. **Proof you are not using?** default: a few nice messages and screenshots never used. Unknown for Ridge.

## The five customers

default: none. Hypothetical situations the builder has seen: the man whose back-pocket wallet ruined a suit; the gift buyer at a loss two weeks before a birthday; the traveler who lost a card to a skimmer; the new father simplifying what he carries; the collector who buys the finish, not the wallet.
