How to Submit a Warranty Claim

To be eligible for a replacement under this warranty, you must submit a claim through the warranty claim process at ridge.com. See the Submit a Warranty Claim section at the top of this page for claim links and gift recipient instructions. If you purchased your Covered Item(s) from a Ridge authorized retailer or partner, proof of purchase may be required for lost or stolen replacements. You may also be required to provide photographs of the claimed damage.

Once a claim is submitted, Ridge will evaluate the information provided. If approved, Ridge will provide replacement parts or a full replacement as outlined in the Warranty Limits by Product table. Processing fees apply for full replacements. If your item's style is no longer available at the time of replacement, you will be offered an alternative item of similar or reasonably equivalent style as identified by Ridge.

What This Warranty Does Not Cover

The Built for Life Warranty does not cover:

Products purchased from unauthorized sellers, including but not limited to Facebook Marketplace and eBay

Products purchased before October 14th, 2025

Complimentary products received through promotional activities

Products with altered, defaced, or removed serial numbers, or that have been materially altered by an unauthorized third party prior to sale  

Damage resulting from unauthorized modifications, disassembly, or repairs performed after purchase

Damage caused by failure to follow product safety and care instructions

Damage from vandalism, fire, flood, or other external causes  

Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality, including scratches, dents, and discoloration  

Ridge's Right to Deny Claims

Ridge reserves the right to deny any Built for Life Warranty claim. This may occur if Ridge believes the claim to be fraudulent, if the product was purchased before October 14th, 2025, if the product is not covered by the Built for Life Warranty, or if the product was purchased from an unauthorized seller such as, but not limited to, Facebook Marketplace and eBay. Items purchased from unauthorized retailers may have been stored improperly, tampered with, or repackaged, and/or may be counterfeit or outdated versions.
