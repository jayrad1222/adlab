Return & Exchange Policy

We want to make returns and exchanges as smooth as possible for you! Here’s what you need to know:

Timeframe: Returns must be initiated through our returns portal within 99 days of your purchase.

Condition: Items should be in usable condition to qualify for a refund or exchange.

Refunds: Refunds go back to your original payment method and can take up to 7 business days once processed.

Exchanges: We’ll ship exchanged items for free within the U.S. after your return is approved.

 

Shipping Labels


U.S. Customers:

You’ll receive a free prepaid USPS label to send your item back.

International Customers: 

Please provide a return label with tracking to this address: 9091 88th Ave, Pleasant Prairie, Wisconsin, 53158, USA

Note: Returns without tracking can’t be refunded.
A Few Extra Things to Know


Non-Ridge Items: Be sure to only send Ridge products – we can’t process non-Ridge items.

Complete Returns: If your order included promotional or free items, please return everything to get a full refund.

Ineligible Items: Personalized items, warranty claim orders and certain limited edition items aren’t eligible for returns or refunds.

Shipping Costs: Original shipping fees aren’t refundable.

Drop-Off Receipts: We recommend keeping your drop-off receipt for peace of mind. If there’s an issue with your return label or shipping, we’ll work with you to sort it out.

For additional information or questions, please feel free to reach out to us through our support widget.
