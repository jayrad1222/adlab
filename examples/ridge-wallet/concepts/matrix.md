Written 2026-10-23 for `/adlab:angles` from the five persona files, `personas/coverage.md` (with its addendum), `analysis/market-map.md`, `analysis/conventions.md`, `business/interrogation.md` and the pages captured for this step (`business/brief.md`, addendum: the Returns & Exchanges page, the Warranty Policy page and the Ridge Wallet 2.0 product page). Ridge is a stand-in member; nothing is owner-provided. `assets/catalog.md` exists with 0 rows, so no photograph, clip or quote is available as proof; what an ad can stand on today is what Ridge's own pages state. Counts are of the 9 roster advertiser Pages, which are 6 brands (three Pages are Ridge's own and one is a Bellroy retailer); `SAT n/b` gives both, and the table-stakes counts follow the market map's corrected section 3. Ridge is inside most counts, so they partly describe Ridge. Nothing here is performance data; every cell is a hypothesis.

## How to read a cell

- `proof → format`: usable now. Proof codes: `situation` (names a moment, makes no claim), `terms` (the trial and warranty terms as the captured pages state them, observed), `spec` (product page spec lines, observed: "Holds 1-12 Cards", "Swappable Cash Strap or Money Clip", "RFID-Blocking Plates"), `site` (other site lines, observed). `insight` marks a cell whose hook rests on the five featured reviews (observed customer language; used as insight, never as ad copy without the reviewer's consent).
- `held (needs X) → format`: usable once the named asset exists and is cataloged with consent, or once the named owner decision is made. The concept can be written; it cannot be `ready`.
- `rej: CODE`: rejected, with the reason.
  - `NP` no proof available (needs a customer result, a testimonial with consent, a sourced number, or a photograph that does not exist).
  - `PR` policy risk (a personal attribute put on the viewer, fear-first framing, a before-and-after implying a body or money change, an unverifiable superlative; `library/platform/meta-policy-pitfalls.md`, `library/platform/ftc-endorsements.md`).
  - `ST` wrong stage for this persona.
  - `SAT n/b` crowded: n of 9 Pages, b brands, already run this angle at this persona.
  - `OWN` needs an owner decision Ridge has not made (pricing, production commitments).
- **OPEN** marks a cell the market leaves empty (market map section 4 uncontested claims, section 5 persona coverage, section 8 format gaps).
- `→ cNN` marks the cell a concept fills.

Format slugs follow `library/copy/format-taxonomy.md`: `static-problem-card`, `static-text-only-ugly`, `static-three-reasons`, `static-faq-card`, `static-us-vs-them`, `static-offer-card`, `static-photo-hook`, `video-demo`, `video-pov`, `video-mini-doc`, `video-how-its-made`, `carousel-tutorial-steps`.

## Cells the market leaves open

From market map sections 4, 5 and 8:

- The switch moment (p1): 0 of 9 Pages; the ritual of moving cards out of the old wallet is uncontested. One featured review describes it ("it took me a couple of days to actually convert", observed, insight only).
- The gadget skeptic (p3): 0 of 9 Pages; "what falls out, and what does not" is uncontested; the 99-day trial exists for it and no ad tells it.
- The professional's fixed carry (p4): 0 of 9 for men; the suit pocket is absent from every roster ad.
- The giver (p5): 0 of 9 Pages; Ridge's own gift ads select by fandom.
- The metal after years (p2): nobody shows it; Bellroy shows leather at day 1,000 (1046004753909784, days active a fact, not a verdict).
- The warranty's terms: 4 of 6 brands (6 of 9 Pages, Ridge's three included) claim a warranty, trial or guarantee (market map s3, corrected); none states what it costs or excludes. Ridge's own page states a $17.95 fee for loss or accidental damage, two replacements at most, and no cover for scratches (observed).
- Formats nobody uses: founder note, text-only ugly, a day counter for metal, two-pockets comparison for wallets, a problem-first static for wallets (market map s8).

## Evidence limits

- The asset catalog has 0 rows: no photograph, clip or consented quote exists for this run. Every visual a concept needs is a planned shoot, written as a held cell, never as proof.
- Customer language is five featured reviews and one AI-generated review summary from one product page, chosen by the page; they inform hooks and are never quoted in an ad without consent.
- The terms are as captured on 2026-10-23 and say they are "subject to change at the discretion of Ridge". Every ad that states them carries that date in its brief and is rechecked before it runs.
- The list price at capture was $95 for Aluminum Black, shown at $76 during a sale; finance.md keeps 110 as a placeholder until the member names the hero product.
- Saturation counts are Pages in an active-only, capped sample; 9 Pages are 6 brands.
