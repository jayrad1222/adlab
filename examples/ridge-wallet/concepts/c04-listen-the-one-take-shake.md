## The one idea

One phone take, sound on, no music: the loaded wallet shaken upside down, the money clip slid along a phone screen, the band pulled, the cards pushed out, with eight cards and then two, so the doubt is answered by the object, whatever it shows.

## The mechanism

All inferred; every effect below is a hypothesis until a readout.

- The psychological job: he must see for himself what happens before any claim lands. His identity is "not taken in by clever products" (persona p3, emotional identity, inferred), so the ad lets him check rather than asks him to believe.
- Make the invisible visible (`library/psychology/make-the-invisible-visible.md`): show the behaviour, not the claim.
- The question nobody asks (`library/psychology/question-nobody-asks.md`): why would he not buy? Because the cards might slide out and the clip might scratch. The take answers the doubt he would not voice.
- Satisficing (`library/psychology/satisficing.md`): remove the catastrophe (a card on a restaurant floor, a scratched phone) before selling anything.
- The pratfall effect (`library/psychology/shotton-choice-factory.md`): showing the push needed to get cards out, a known complaint, buys belief for the rest.
- Native register (`library/strategy/hott-ugly-ads.md`): phone-shot, natural sound, no music, no lower-thirds.
- Copy move: captions name each action as it happens, in the order the take films it, and never its outcome; the uncut take is the only claim.

## Six hooks

1. (`demonstration`) "Eight cards under a Ridge wallet's elastic. Upside down. Shaken. One take."
2. (`specific-number`) "Eight cards, then two, under the same Ridge elastic. The same shake, no cuts."
3. (`question`) "Upside down, do the cards stay in a Ridge? Eight cards, then two, uncut."
4. (`negative-or-warning`) "Before buying a Ridge, watch it shaken upside down with eight cards in. One take, no cuts."
5. (`confession-or-pratfall`) "Getting the cards out takes a push through the cutout. Here is the push, uncut."
6. (`pov`) "POV: the money clip slid along a phone screen, close up, in one take."

Each hook names an action, not an outcome, and follows the take's order (eight cards first, then two). If the take shows a card moving at two cards, that frame stays in and hook 3 is answered on screen as it happened; hook 5 runs only if the take shows the push. Three tests: each hook pictures an object and an action (the elastic, the cutout, the clip on a phone screen); "one take, no cuts" is checkable against the raw file, which is the claim hook 4 makes; and each line names a Ridge or one of Ridge Wallet 2.0's own parts (the elastic, the cutout, the money clip), so it describes this wallet and no other card holder.
