## Rows not run, and why

- Superlatives ("best wallet ever", "#1 bestselling"; conventions row 2, 2 Pages) and rented authority (conventions row 6, Ekster only): the inverse, a small checkable claim, is carried by c02 and c06.
- Finish drops and licences as identity (conventions row 7, Ridge and Thread): its inverse, identity through use, needs a real owner with consent; it waits with c07.

## What this pass rejected and why

- "Never say slim" (row 1, literal): slim is the buyer's search word; dropping it costs legibility for p1.
- "Made for as long as you ask" (row 2, literal): a production commitment no captured page makes.
- A struck-price-free ad during a live sale (row 5): not rejected as an idea, but held, because the ad would contradict the page it lands on.
